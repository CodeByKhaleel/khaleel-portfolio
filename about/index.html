A little about me

Curiosity first. Then a solution that fits.

I’m Khaleel, a Data Engineer who enjoys getting close to a problem before deciding how to build around it.

The through-line

Real-world data work sparked an interest in the engineering behind reliable, reusable workflows.

I began by helping collect and check university data. That work grew into scraping and automation workflows, where each source had its own structure and exceptions.

While building a configurable submission workflow, I became especially interested in the data moving through it: extracting records from a CRM, transforming them into the shape a product needed, and loading them into the workflow.

I’m already a Data Engineer by role. Now I’m deepening my skills toward broader, larger-scale data engineering, while building on the practical experience I have in extraction, ETL, data quality, and automation.

What I value: understanding the constraints and edge cases before writing the solution.
